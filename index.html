About

My research focuses on banking, financial markets, and macroeconomic dynamics, with particular emphasis on emerging economies.

A central strand of my work examines banking and credit markets, including household and corporate indebtedness, credit risk, debt restructuring, financial regulation, and the implications of these issues for financial stability and policy. I am also involved in research on labor market dynamics, earnings processes, and employment transitions, exploring their implications for household behavior and aggregate economic outcomes.

These research interests are complemented by work in quantitative macroeconomics, particularly heterogeneous-agent models, monetary policy, and the determinants of aggregate economic dynamics. More broadly, my research seeks to understand how financial institutions, market frictions, and individual economic decisions shape aggregate outcomes.
